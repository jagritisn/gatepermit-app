# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Visitors who need to enter a government office — a broad public audience, not a specialist one. Many are unfamiliar with the system, some are anxious about bureaucratic process, and tech comfort varies widely (from smartphone-native to low-tech-familiarity). They arrive with a concrete goal and limited patience for friction: they want to get in and get their business done.

## Product Purpose

A responsive web app that handles the full visitor journey for entering a government office: requesting an entry permit, tracking its status, and presenting the approved pass at the gate. Success looks like a visitor completing a request in under a couple of minutes without confusion, always knowing what state their request is in, and being able to show/verify their pass at the entrance with zero friction.

## Brand Personality

Efficient, minimal, contemporary. The interface should feel like a well-run service counter, not a paper form digitized as-is: quiet confidence, no decoration for its own sake, nothing that slows the visitor down.

## Anti-references

- The typical dense, dated, bureaucratic government portal: small text, cluttered forms, unclear steps, unclear which step you're on.
- Consumer-app gloss: playful illustrations, marketing-style flourishes, anything that feels like a lifestyle app rather than an official service.

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over cleverness** — every screen makes the next step obvious; visitors are often stressed or unfamiliar with the process.
2. **One task at a time** — requesting a permit and presenting an approved pass are two distinct jobs; keep them visually and structurally separate rather than conflating them.
3. **Trust through precision** — credibility for a government service comes from crisp, unambiguous execution, not visual flourish.
4. **Accessible by default** — plain language, generous touch targets, and multi-language support are baseline requirements, not enhancements.
5. **Fast completion** — minimize steps and fields; speed and efficiency are the core promise of the product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance (contrast, keyboard navigation, screen reader support).
- Design for low-literacy / low-tech-familiarity users: simple language, large touch targets, minimal steps per screen.
- Multi-language / i18n support for the visitor-facing flow.
