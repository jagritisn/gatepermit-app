# Jobs-to-be-done: Approving Officer

See [`product/roles/approving-officer.md`](../roles/approving-officer.md) for the full role profile.

1. When new permit requests come in, I want to see them in a clear, prioritized queue, so I can review and act on every one without missing any.

2. When I'm reviewing a request, I want to see the visitor's simulated identity and stated purpose at a glance, so I can make a fast, informed approve/deny decision.

3. When a request looks incomplete or unclear, I want to flag it or request more information, so I don't approve something I'm unsure about.

4. When I approve or deny a request, I want the visitor notified automatically, so I don't have to communicate the decision by hand.

5. When my past decisions are questioned, I want an audit trail of what I approved or denied and why, so I can account for my actions.
