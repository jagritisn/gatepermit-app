# Jobs-to-be-done: Visitor

See [`product/roles/visitor.md`](../roles/visitor.md) for the full role profile.

1. When I need to visit a government office for official business, I want to request an entry permit quickly online, so I can avoid waiting in an unclear line or being turned away at the gate.

2. When I've submitted a permit request, I want to check its approval status at any time, so I can plan my visit with confidence instead of wondering.

3. When my permit is approved, I want to access my pass easily on my phone, so I can present it at the gate without hunting for paperwork.

4. When I arrive at the gate, I want to be verified in seconds, so I can enter without delay or standing around confused.

5. When something goes wrong with my digital pass — no signal, a dead phone — I want a manual fallback option, so I'm not blocked from entering despite having a valid, approved permit.
